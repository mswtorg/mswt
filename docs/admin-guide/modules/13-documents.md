# Documents

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

Registration certificates, annual reports and audited accounts, shown as public PDFs on the **Transparency** page. Donors and funders use them to trust the Trust.

![Documents list](img/documents-list.jpg)

## Adding a document

1. **Documents → New document.** Choose the **Type** (annual report, audited accounts, a registration certificate such as 12A, 80G, Trust registration or PAN, or other), the **Financial year** (e.g. 2025-26; leave empty for certificates) and status.
2. **Create document**, then upload the PDF.
3. Add the title in each language, e.g. "Annual report 2025-26", and tick reviewed.

![A new document](img/documents-new.jpg)

## Before uploading

- Everything here is **public**. Remove or black out bank account numbers, signatures you don't want copied, and any donor's name or PAN.
- Upload only final, approved versions.
- Deleting a document removes its PDF too.
