# Users

Who can use it: Super Admin

Who can sign in to the admin, and with which role.

![Users](img/users.jpg)

## Inviting someone

1. **Users → Invite a user:** full name, email and role.
2. **Create invite link.** Copy the one-time link and send it to them yourself (by WhatsApp or email). It is valid for 72 hours.
3. They choose a password and set up two-step verification (see "Signing in and your account").

Give each person the smallest role they need: Accountant for finance, Content Editor for the website.

## Looking after an account

Open the person's name:

- **Change role:** their sessions end when it changes.
- **Create reset link** (or **Create a new invite link** if they never finished signing up): a one-time link, valid for 72 hours, for a forgotten password or an expired invite.
- **Reset two-step verification:** if they lost their phone and recovery codes. Confirm it is really them first (call them).
- **Deactivate account:** when someone leaves the Trust. They can't sign in, but their history stays in the audit log. Do this on their last day. **Reactivate account** undoes it.
- **Recent changes:** their last 10 audit entries.

Keep at least two Super Admins, so the Trust is never locked out.
