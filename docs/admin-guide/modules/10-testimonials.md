# Testimonials

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

Stories from donors, volunteers and families. Each testimonial has its own page under /testimonials; Home and About show cards that link to it.

![Testimonials list](img/testimonials-list.jpg)

## Adding a testimonial

1. **Testimonials → New testimonial.** Settings: web address (e.g. `asha-school-fees`), status, date, order, photo, and **Show on the home page**.
2. **Create testimonial**, then for each language write:
   - **Name:** a first name or initials is fine, especially for children and families.
   - **Who they are:** optional, e.g. "Parent, Mumbai".
   - **Heading:** the title of the story.
   - **Short summary:** one or two sentences for the cards.
   - **Story:** the full text.
3. Tick reviewed and **Save text**.

![A new testimonial](img/testimonials-new.jpg)

Home shows the first 6 ticked for the home page; About shows the first 6 of all; the Testimonials page shows every one. Lower Order numbers come first.

Always have the person's consent before publishing their story or photo.
