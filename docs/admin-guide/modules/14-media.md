# Media

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

The photo library used by pages, causes, events, news, team and the gallery. Upload a photo once and use it anywhere.

![The media library](img/media-list.jpg)

## Uploading

1. **Media → Upload a photo**, or **Upload new** in any photo field.
2. Add **alt text**: a short description of the photo for people who use screen readers, e.g. "Children receiving school bags at the Pune centre".
3. After uploading, add alt text in the other languages the photo appears in.

The site makes smaller, faster copies automatically and removes hidden location data (EXIF) from the photo.

## Rules for photos

- Each photo needs alt text in every language it appears in; the admin won't mark text reviewed until it has.
- Use photos the Trust owns or has permission to use.
- Be careful with children's photos: avoid identifying details.
- A photo's page shows where it is used. Remove it from those places before deleting it.
