# Team

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

Board members and team. Everyone published is on the **Team** page (/team) in each language they are written in. Members ticked **Show on the About page** are also shown on About.

![Team list](img/team-list.jpg)

## Adding a member

1. **Team → New team member.** Settings: status, order (lower first), photo (shown round; without one, initials are shown) and **Show on the About page**.
2. **Add member**, then for each language write the **Name**, **Designation** (e.g. Managing Trustee, Treasurer) and **Details**.
3. Tick reviewed and **Save text**.

![Editing a team member](img/team-edit.jpg)

## On the public site

Each person is a card with photo, name and designation. Hovering with the mouse, or pressing **About <name>**, turns the card over to show the details.

![The public Team page](img/public-team.jpg)

## Things to remember

- Members appear in Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati only once their text in that language is added and reviewed.
- To add the Team page to the header or footer, use **Menus**.
- Deleting a member removes them in every language. To hide someone for a while, set them to Draft instead.
