# Reports

Who can use it: Super Admin and Accountant

**Reports** gives the financial-year figures the Trust and its CA need, as CSV or Excel. A financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March (India time).

![Reports](img/reports.jpg)

## What you can download

Choose the financial year (and, for some, a cause), then **CSV** or **Excel**:

- **FY donation register:** every paid donation with its receipt, donor and payment details.
- **Cause-wise summary:** totals per cause.
- **Mode-wise summary:** totals per payment mode (online, cash, cheque, transfer, UPI).
- **80G receipt register:** 80G receipts only, for the Form 10BD filing.
- **Acknowledgement register:** acknowledgements (no PAN, cash above ₹2,000, non-80G causes).

## PAN in reports

PAN is masked in downloads unless you unlock full PANs first: **Email me a code**, type the code from the email and **Show full PANs**. They stay visible for 15 minutes, and every unlock is recorded in the audit log. Unlock only when preparing the 10BD return or when the CA needs it.

## Good habits

- Keep downloaded files in a safe, shared place the Trust controls, not on personal phones.
- Delete local copies with full PANs once they have been used.
