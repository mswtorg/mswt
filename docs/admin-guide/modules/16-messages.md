# Messages

Who can use it: everyone (handling: Super Admin and Content Editor)

Messages sent through the site's Contact form. They are kept until an admin deletes them. A copy also arrives by email to the alert recipients.

![Messages](img/messages.jpg)

## Handling a message

1. Open the message. You see the sender's name, email, phone, language and the text.
2. Reply from your normal email app.
3. Back in the admin, set **Status** to Replied (the other statuses are New, Read and Spam) and **Assigned to** the person handling it, then **Save**.

Use the filters (**Status**, **Assigned to**, **Search**) to see what is still open.

## Deleting

**Delete message** removes it for good. The audit log keeps who deleted it and when, not the text. Delete spam, and messages no longer needed.
