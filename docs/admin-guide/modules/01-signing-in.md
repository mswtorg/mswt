# Signing in and your account

Who can use it: everyone

Every admin uses a password **and** a code from an authenticator app on their phone (two-step verification). There is no way to switch this off.

## Your first sign-in

1. The Super Admin sends you a one-time invite link (valid for 72 hours). Open it and choose a password of at least 12 characters. A few random words work well.
2. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 2FAS.
3. Scan the QR code shown on screen (or type the key), then enter the 6-digit code the app shows.
4. You are shown **10 recovery codes once**. Print them or write them down and keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone. Each code works once if you lose your phone.

## Every sign-in after that

1. Go to **mswelfaretrust.org/admin** and enter your email and password.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If you don't have your phone, use one of your recovery codes instead.

## Staying safe

- **After 2 hours without activity you are signed out.** While you are typing in a form, the admin keeps you signed in, so you don't lose work.
- **After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked** for a while. Wait, or ask the Super Admin.
- Use **Sign out** (the arrow next to your name, bottom left) on shared computers.
- Never share your password, your phone codes or your recovery codes, not even with another trustee. Each person has their own account so the audit log shows who did what.

## My account

Open **My account** at the bottom of the menu.

![My account](img/account.jpg)

- **Password:** enter your current password and the new one twice, then **Change password**.
- **Recovery codes:** used them up or lost them? **Make new recovery codes**. The old ones stop working at once.

## If you lose your phone

Sign in with a recovery code, then ask the Super Admin to **Reset two-step verification** for you (Users → your name). At your next sign-in you scan a new QR code.
