# Pages

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

**Pages** holds the site's fixed pages: Home, About, Contact, Transparency, the legal pages, and the headings of the list pages (Causes, Events, News, Gallery, Testimonials, Team). Each has its own entry under Pages in the menu.

![The list of pages](img/pages-list.jpg)

## Editing a page

![Editing the Home page](img/pages-home.jpg)

1. Open the page under **Pages**.
2. Choose the language tab. The dot beside each language says whether its text is reviewed.
3. Change the text. Each page has its own sections; for example Home has header photos, heading, "Why give", "Impact in numbers" and the other home sections. About has "Our story".
4. Tick **Reviewed by a person: publish in this language** when the text is ready.
5. **Save**, then **View page** to check it.

Header photos are the same in every language. Choose one photo, or several for a slider, and the photo size (short banner, standard or tall).

![Editing the About page](img/pages-about.jpg)

## "(list)" pages

Entries such as **Causes (list)** or **Team (list)** hold only the heading, introduction and search-engine text of that list page. The items themselves (each cause, each team member) are edited in their own section of the menu.

## Search engines

Each page has an **SEO title** and **SEO description**: what Google shows in its results. Keep the description to about 150 characters. Empty fields use the page title and introduction.

## Legal text

Privacy, terms and refund pages are legal text. Change them only with the trustees' approval.
