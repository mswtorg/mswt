# News

Who can use it: Super Admin and Content Editor

News posts are updates and stories from the Trust: what happened at a camp, a thank-you, an announcement. Newest posts come first.

![News list](img/news-list.jpg)

## Writing a post

1. **News → New post.** Choose the web address, status, date, cover photo and **Author (optional)**.
2. Save, then write the title, summary and text in each language.
3. In the text editor you can add headings, lists, links and photos from the library.
4. Tick reviewed and save, then view the post.

![Editing a news post](img/news-edit.jpg)

## Good habits

- Use real photos the Trust has permission to publish. For children, avoid full names and faces where possible.
- Every photo needs alt text (a short description for people who can't see it), in each language the post is in.
