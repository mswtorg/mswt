# Donors

Who can use it: Super Admin and Accountant (merging: Super Admin)

**Donors** lists everyone who has donated, newest activity first. One donor can have many donations.

![Donors list](img/donors-list.jpg)

## A donor's page

![A donor's page](img/donors-detail.jpg)

- **Record:** masked PAN, address, when they were added, whether they declared they are an Indian citizen, and marketing consent.
- **Donations by this donor**, each linking to the donation.
- **Contact details:** correct the name, email, mobile or address for future emails. Receipts already issued keep the old details; to change a receipt, reissue it from the donation's page.

The PAN can only be changed by reissuing a receipt, so the receipt and the record always agree.

## Merging duplicates (Super Admin)

Sometimes the same person appears twice (for example with two email addresses).

1. Open the record you want to remove.
2. In **Merge duplicate**, paste the address of the other donor's page (or their ID).
3. **Merge into that donor.** All donations move to the other record and this one is hidden.

Donors with different PANs can't be merged.
