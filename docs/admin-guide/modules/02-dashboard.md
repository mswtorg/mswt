# Dashboard

Who can use it: everyone (money figures: Super Admin and Accountant)

The Dashboard is the first screen after signing in. It is a quick look at how things stand today.

![Dashboard](img/dashboard.jpg)

## What it shows

- **Today:** donations received today (India time) and their total.
- **Waiting for payment:** online donations started but not yet paid. Most are donors who closed the payment page. Unpaid orders become "expired" after 3 days; nothing needs doing.
- **Last reconciliation:** when the daily check with Cashfree last ran (03:00 IST). It asks Cashfree about any payment the site is unsure of, so a missed confirmation is fixed automatically.
- **Donations:** recent totals by cause and mode, with a **Record offline donation** shortcut.
- **Latest messages** from the contact form, and **Your sections**: shortcuts to what your role can edit.

Content Editors don't see the money figures.

## Good habits

- Glance at the Dashboard once a day.
- If the nightly check misses a day, a yellow warning appears at the top of the Dashboard ("The nightly check hasn't finished since …"), and the alert recipients get a **[MSWT] [Job failed]** email. Tell the developer.
